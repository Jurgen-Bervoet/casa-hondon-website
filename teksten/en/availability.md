## Pricing & deposit

- Your booking is confirmed after an initial deposit of 25% of the total rental sum.
- The remaining balance is due 6 weeks before arrival.
- A security deposit of €1,000 is due no later than 1 week before your stay begins.
- Energy costs are included up to a standard usage (indicatively €8/day electricity, €5/day
  water); usage above this is deducted from the security deposit.
- Cleaning fee: €160, mandatory per booking.

## Cancellation policy

- Cancelling 6 weeks or more before arrival: 25% of the rental price
- Cancelling 4 weeks or more before arrival: 50% of the rental price
- Cancelling 2 weeks or more before arrival: 100% of the rental price

The owner is not responsible for accidents or theft.
