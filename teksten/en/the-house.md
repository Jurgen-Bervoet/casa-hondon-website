## Intro

Casa Lana is cosy and complete: large enough to enjoy with company, small enough to feel
the calm of home in every corner. Inside, every comfort; outside, a terrace and pool where
you'll spend most of your time.

## Features

- 3 spacious bedrooms, 2 bathrooms
- Fully standalone house, no neighbours nearby
- Private pool, maintained weekly
- Large, fully fenced plot
- Fully equipped kitchen
- Large garage that fits your car

## Amenities

- Wifi
- Air conditioning
- Pellet stove
- Bed linen and towels provided
- Sun loungers
- Fully equipped kitchen with cutlery, plates, glasses and cups
- Coffee maker, juicer, kettle, microwave, oven, freezer and fridge
- Vacuum cleaner and hair dryer
- BBQ
- Free parking in the garage

To bring yourself: beach towels.
