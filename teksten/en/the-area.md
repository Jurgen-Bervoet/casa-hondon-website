## Intro

Hondón de las Nieves lies in the green inland countryside of Alicante province, in the valley
it shares with its neighbouring village, Hondón de los Frailes. Mountains, almond trees,
vineyards and the well-known salt lakes shape the landscape — a very different Spain from the
busy coast.

## Things to do

**Hiking & nature**
The surrounding mountains offer hiking routes for every level, with views over the valley.

**The salt lakes**
A striking natural area between the two Hondón villages, popular with birdwatchers and
photographers.

**The village**
About a 10-minute drive away is Hondón de las Nieves itself: a quiet, authentic village with
good restaurants and a cosy breakfast café.

**Local produce**
The region is known for its almonds, olive oil and wine — ideal for lovers of local
gastronomy.
